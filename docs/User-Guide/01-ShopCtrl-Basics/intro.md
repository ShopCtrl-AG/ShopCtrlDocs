# Welcome to ShopCtrl

ShopCtrl is a platform for managing e-commerce operations — from order processing and fulfillment to customer communication, product management, and purchasing.

This guide covers the setup, configuration, and day-to-day use of the platform.

## What's in this guide

- **ShopCtrl Setup** — Organization settings, employee accounts and permissions, sales channel connections, customer management, and fulfillment configuration.
- **Basic Functions** — Alerts, reporting, and reference documentation.
- **Order Management** — Orders, offers, contracts, allocation, and bulk operations.
- **Financial** — Invoices, credit notes, and accounting integrations.
- **Communication** — Messaging, tickets, and customer support tools.
- **Fulfillment** — Shipments, parcels, tracking, dropshipments, and returns.
- **Product Management** — Products, variants, stock, warehouses, and product import/export.
- **Purchasing** — Suppliers, purchase orders, and deliveries.
