# Invoice Reminders

ShopCtrl can follow up unpaid invoices for you by sending payment reminders automatically. You decide:

* **When** each reminder is sent — for example 2, 14 and 21 days after the due date
* **What** each reminder contains — using mail templates with [merge fields](/User-Guide/02-ShopCtrl-Setup/02-General-Setup/04-Templates/Email-Templates/merge-fields-in-email-templates.md) and your own document templates
* **Who** receives it — the customer, yourself, or both
* **What else happens** — for example, create a task or a ticket, or run a trigger

If you turn on the [order warning](#general-reminder-settings), employees also see the warning *No new orders allowed until overdue invoices are paid!* on all orders of a customer. The warning appears once a reminder of the selected step has been sent for one of the customer's open invoices.

<img src={require("/img/order-overdue-invoice-warning.png").default} height="" width="800" />

## Reminder steps

You can set up to five steps per shop:

1. **Pre expiration reminder** — an email sent a number of days before the due date
2. **1st reminder**
3. **2nd reminder**
4. **3rd reminder**
5. **After expiration mail** — an email sent a number of days after the due date

For the 1st, 2nd and 3rd reminder you choose an action:

* **Create task** — creates a task, such as a phone call, for a team member
* **Create ticket** — creates a ticket with a set category for an employee group
* **Execute trigger** — runs the matching reminder trigger, for example to call a webhook
* **Send email** — sends an email based on a mail template

For each reminder you can also select a **document template**, for example an invoice with a due notice.

## Set up automatic reminders

:::info[**Required Permissions**]

An employee must be assigned to the shop with a **Shop Admin** or **Shop Owner Admin** role.

:::

1. Open the shop settings: right-click the shop name in the sales channel pane and select **Settings**.
2. Go to **Financial > Invoice**.
<img src={require("/img/shop-settings-invoice.png").default} height="" width="800" />
3. Set up the reminder steps you need, as described below.
4. In the **Reminders** pane, clear the **Disable auto generation** checkbox to start sending reminders.
5. Click **Save and close**.

The reminders are generated by a scheduled task that runs once a day, usually at night. The first reminders are sent the next day, unless you agree on an earlier start with ShopCtrl support.

### Pre expiration reminder

* **Days before expiration** — when the email is sent
* **Mail template** — the email to send

### General reminder settings

In the **Reminders** pane:

* **Send the original invoice with a reminder** — attaches the original invoice to reminder emails
* **Additional days for the 1st reminder** — payment tolerance, to give a payment made on the due date time to arrive
* **Order warning** — whether employees see a warning on the orders of a customer with an unpaid reminded invoice. The default is **Do not show warning**. To turn the warning on, select when it starts: **Show warning when 1st reminder active**, **2nd reminder active** or **3rd reminder active**. Reminders sent manually also count.
* **Employee group for ticket** and **Ticket category** (optional) — used when a reminder step creates a ticket

In the **Restrict reminder generation** pane, set **Generate after invoice date** to send reminders only for invoices from this date onwards.

<img src={require("/img/invoice-reminders-settings.png").default} height="" width="800" />

### 1st, 2nd and 3rd reminder

For each reminder:

* **Action** — what happens when the reminder is due
* **Document template** (optional) — a custom invoice document for this reminder
* **Reminder expires in days** — when the next step starts
* **Mail template** — the email to send, if the action is **Send email**

<img src={require("/img/invoice-reminders-steps.png").default} height="" width="800" />

### After expiration mail

* **Days after expiration** — when the email is sent
* **Mail template** — the email to send
* **Main order status** — the status the order gets when the invoice has expired

## Send a reminder manually

You can create a reminder for an invoice at any moment from the invoice details page. You can change the mail and document template and edit the email before you send it.
