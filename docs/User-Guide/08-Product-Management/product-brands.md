# Product Brands 



Product brands can be managed from Product Management > Product Brands.

Product brands grid gives an overview of all brands synced or created for your products.
<img src={require("/img/product-brands-overview.png").default} height="" width="800" />

You can **edit**, **add** new and **copy** existing brands. The filter is set to **Enabled Brands** by default.

## How to create a new product brand

<img src={require("/img/create-product-brand.png").default} height="" width="800" />

To create a new brand within ShopCtrl and then sync to the webshops:
1. Open Product Management > Product Brands.
2. Click **Add** and select a shop group for which you would like to create a brand. A new product brand page will be opened in a new tab.
3. Fill in **Name**.
4. Activate the **Enabled** checkbox to be able to choose this brand for products.
5. (Optional) Upload a Logo.
6. Click **Save** or **Save and Close**.

## How to edit product brand

<img src={require("/img/edit-product-brand.png").default} height="" width="800" />

To **edit** a brand, go to Product Management > Product Brands and double click on the brand name row. Alternatively, within the Product details page > General tab > Miscellaneous, click the <img src={require("/img/simple-go.png").default}  /> icon in the product brand field.

You can see all of the specific brand products listed in the Products section. Click on the icon in the product row to open the product details page on a new tab.
You can also select a product row and click the **Delete** button in the products pane to unlink a certain product from a brand.

Once the brand is no more in use, you can **disable** it so that it won't appear in the brand list. You can also **delete** the brand. 

:::warning[**Please note**]

If the brand is still linked to one or more products, it is not possible to delete it. 

:::
