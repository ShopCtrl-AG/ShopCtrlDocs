# Email Templates

:::info[**Required permissions**]

An employee must be assigned to the Shop with a **Power User** role or higher to create and edit templates on a Shop Owner level.

:::

## Overview

Set up your email templates that will be automatically sent each time a customer orders a product, a shipment is created, or goods are purchased from a supplier. Create email templates on different subjects to choose from when you write to customers or suppliers.

### Kinds of email templates

The mail templates are divided by types to be used on various occasions and displayed in selecting lists properly.

| Addressed to | Type                            | Description                             |
| ------------ | ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| For Customer | General                         | General topic customer emails           |
| For Customer | Offer                           | Autogeneration of offers emails         |
| For Customer | Order                           | Order confirmation emails               |
| For Customer | Invoice                         | Invoice issuing emails                  |
| For Customer | Credit Invoice                  | Credit Invoice issuing emails           |
| For Customer | Invoice Reminder 1              | First invoice reminder email            |
| For Customer | Invoice Reminder 2              | Second invoice reminder email           |
| For Customer | Invoice Reminder 3              | Third invoice reminder email            |
| For Customer | Pre Expiration Invoice Reminder | Pre expiration invoice reminder email   |
| For Customer | After Expiration Invoice Mail   | After expiration invoice reminder email |
| For Customer | OrderShipment                   | Shipment confirmation email             |
| For Customer | Service Contract                | Service contract email template         |
| For Customer | Order Return                    | Order return email template             |
| For Supplier | Purchase Order                  | Emails sent with a purchase order       |
| For Supplier | Stock Confirm                   | Stock confirmation emails               |
| Generic      | NonCustomer                     | Free to use                             |
